AI Resume Screening & Ranking

A smart web-based system designed for companies, recruiters, and organizations to automatically screen, analyze, and rank resumes using Artificial Intelligence.

How the System Works

📄 Resume Upload

Companies or recruiters can upload multiple resumes in PDF or DOCX format. The system automatically extracts relevant textual information.

🤖 AI-Based Screening

Using Natural Language Processing (NLP), resumes are analyzed and compared with job requirements to identify skill relevance.

📊 Candidate Ranking

Resumes are ranked based on similarity scores and relevance, helping decision-makers shortlist the most suitable candidates.

Who Can Use This Platform?

🏢 Companies & HR Teams

Reduce manual resume screening effort and speed up the hiring process while maintaining consistency and fairness.

🧑‍💼 Recruiters

Quickly shortlist candidates from large resume pools and focus more on interviews and decision-making.

🎓 Institutions & Projects

Can be used as a learning tool or prototype to demonstrate AI-driven automation in recruitment workflows.

Why Use AI Resume Screening?

⏱ Time Efficient

Automatically process hundreds of resumes in seconds, saving valuable time for recruiters and HR teams.

⚖ Unbiased Ranking

Focuses on skills and relevance rather than manual judgment, helping reduce unconscious bias.

📈 Scalable Solution

Suitable for startups as well as large organizations handling high-volume recruitment.
